      What you are looking at

      Observed
      Actual SPHEREx infrared pixels, or historical NASA planetary image mosaics. Missing pixels stay blank.
      Catalog
      Gaia DR3 measurements, with catalog IDs and distance intervals. The nearby-star sample is incomplete.
      Computed
      Planet positions and orbit paths from Astropy / ERFA; stellar depth from catalog parallax. Planet globes and NASA appearance models are rendered geometry, not live photos.

      All center-to-center distances within each view use one linear scale. Planet radii are enlarged for visibility. Globe spin, map longitude and ring orientation are illustrative. Approximate ephemerides are for exploration, not navigation.

      Solar System and stellar scales are separate: AU for planets, parsecs for stars. There is no known Planet X position to plot.

      Surface maps: NASA/Goddard SVS (Blue Marble), NASA/JPL/USGS (Mariner 10 and Viking), NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute (Cassini); optional appearance models: NASA VTAD. Historical planetary maps are not SPHEREx data.
